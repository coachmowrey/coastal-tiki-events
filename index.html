Questions

Frequently asked.

How much space do you need?

Roughly a 10×10 ft area and a standard outlet within 50 ft for each experience booked.

Do you travel outside Volusia County?

Yes — New Smyrna Beach, Edgewater, Port Orange, Daytona Beach, Ormond Beach and DeLand are home base, but we'll travel further for a mileage fee.

Can we add hours the day of the event?

If our schedule allows, yes — extra time is billed at your package's hourly rate.

What happens if it rains?

We just need a covered, powered backup spot — a tent, porch or indoor room all work.

Do we get to keep our photos and drawings?

Yes — every photo and drawing from your event is included, with no per-print or per-drawing fee.
